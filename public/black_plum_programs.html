Denefits — White Glove Service Center

Denefits is our partner White Glove Service Center for CRM and Receivables. It provides the financing infrastructure that allows us to finance service providers and members into projects in perpetuity, creating sustainability and preventing devaluation of services.

Financing Terms

Available for service agreements and member plans over $300 per month. Payment plans of 3, 6, 9, or 12 months. No interest charged. One-time service fee of $39.

CRM · Receivables

Pre-Approved Integration

Denefits provides a pre-approved link, API, and widget for direct integration into our site. Automated payment processing and receivables management.

Professional Conduct & Protection Policy

This ecosystem is owned and operated by one person — Brandy Bianchini. Any disruption to operations, misrepresentation of roles, or bad business behavior results in documented fees, reported liabilities, and legal action. These are not threats — they are law and your right when a year of time and funding has been affected by manipulative behavior.

Professional Attire Standard

All team members, field agents, and collaborators are required to maintain business casual or business formal dress at all events and meetings. Minimum standard: jacket and slacks. Check specific event requirements in advance.

Non-Negotiable

Investor Meeting Protocol

No partner or peer may speak for Brandy Bianchini, represent her financial role, or include her in investor discussions without prior written approval. Violation results in immediate liability documentation and fees.

Process disrespect, misrepresentation, unauthorized financial inclusion, and manipulation of the ecosystem are billable offenses. Fees range from $500 to $5,000+ per incident. Losses are reported against the project and person.
