Partner & Peer Onboarding Workflow

A 7-stage process from initial legal vetting through full pod/nest activation. No partner, contractor, field rep, or agent goes live until all stages are complete and they have formally said yes.

0
Legal Vetting & Due Diligence Required First ▼
  • Background and reference verification completed
  • Due diligence checklist reviewed and passed
  • No conflicts of interest identified
  • Professional conduct standards reviewed and agreed to
  • Intellectual property protection clause acknowledged
  • Bad Business Behavior policy reviewed and signed
1
Initial Intake & Needs Assessment In Progress ▼
  • First session completed — listening and questions
  • Fill-in-the-blanks needs assessment form submitted
  • Life position and professional goals documented
  • Service agreement scope defined per project and per person
  • Denefits financing education completed (amounts over $300/month, 3–12 month plans, no interest, $39 one-time fee)
2
Education & Ecosystem Orientation Pending ▼
  • Ecosystem overview presented (all 8 brands and their roles)
  • Universal Changemakers, Series L3C mission explained
  • Eternal Autumn Trust role and funding structure explained
  • Financial suite overview provided (in-house tools and Denefits)
  • Rapid Response Plan compliance requirements reviewed
  • Professional attire and conduct standards confirmed (business casual or formal — jacket and slacks)
3
Service Agreement & Contract Execution Pending ▼
  • Service agreement drafted per project and per person
  • Compensation structure agreed and documented
  • Suggestion fee and dues structure confirmed
  • Liability and recovery schedule acknowledged
  • Investor meeting protocol signed (no speaking for Brandy without prior written approval)
  • Contract executed and filed
4
Pod / Nest Build-Out & Tool Integration Pending ▼
  • Odoo CRM profile created and configured
  • Denefits White Glove Service Center account activated
  • BetterWorld fundraising and community tools connected
  • Pietra Studio product and commerce hub set up
  • Wethos Studio project and collaboration workspace configured
  • Resources Center property and product hub access granted
  • Group app and document upload access enabled
  • Real-time collaboration tools activated
5
Alignment & Rapid Response Plan Compliance Pending ▼
  • Rapid Response Plan compliance assessment completed
  • Quintessential map of token components created
  • Existing work incorporated into operations alignment
  • Program assignment confirmed (Programs 1–4)
  • Scope level assignment confirmed (State through Member)
  • Monthly content creation schedule aligned with production schedule
6
Go-Live — Pod Activated & Profile Published Pending ▼
  • Formal "Yes" received and documented
  • Dedicated landing page created and published
  • All tool integrations tested and confirmed live
  • First project or event assigned and scheduled
  • Sustainability financing through Denefits activated
  • Opportunities shared broadly and transparently with the cohort

Property Management & Asset Procurement

Government surplus property procurement strategy. Properties are identified, procured, developed through the programs, and funded via Crowdfunding (CF) regulations to create brick-and-mortar locations for the ecosystem.

Stage 1 — Identify & Procure

1
Access government software and portals to identify surplus property in target cities, states, and counties.
2
Evaluate properties against program needs — hybrid work model (in-person and remote) for team and service providers.
3
Submit procurement application and secure property as an asset.

Stage 2 — Develop Through Programs

1
Assign property to the relevant program (Programs 1–4) for development and activation.
2
Build out the location as a program hub — events, activities, and content production site.
3
Integrate with the GRID (Global Responsible Integrated Development Tool) — minimum 16 cells per state, uncapped squares.

Stage 3 — Capital Raise & Funding

1
Structure capital raise using CF (Crowdfunding) regulations and applicable investment regulations.
2
Activate PRI (Program-Related Investment) and DAF (Donor Advised Fund) funding pathways through Eternal Autumn Trust.
3
Deploy Denefits financing for ongoing operational sustainability and service provider compensation.
